title: Why I'm starting this site
date: 2026-11-09
description: One place for what I think, what I'm building and what I'm reading.
---

I've written a lot over the past few years. Almost all of it lives on someone else's website.

That has been good for the work. [Tech Safari](https://techsafari.beehiiv.com) and Next Capital reach readers I could never reach alone. It also means I have no single place that shows what I think, what I've built and what I'm reading.

This site is that place. I'll use it for three things:

- Essays that don't fit a newsletter.
- Notes on what I'm learning at Imperial and while building Hellodoc.
- A running list of the books I read.

Most posts will be short. When I change my mind about something, I'll update the post and say so.
